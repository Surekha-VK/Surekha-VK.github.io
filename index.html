SIH Project Concept

Problem2Impact — SIH Project

A platform concept designed for crowdsourcing societal challenges and connecting citizens, students, researchers, universities, industries, NGOs, government bodies, and authorized experts.

Core Architectural Features:

  • Citizens can post genuine societal problems.
  • Students, researchers, universities, industries, NGOs, government agencies, and experts can collaborate.
  • Users provide problem explanations, evidence, skills, questions, or proof uploads.
  • AI assists with automated sector categorization and duplicate problem detection.
  • Supports team formation, matching, evaluation, prototyping, testing, and implementation.
